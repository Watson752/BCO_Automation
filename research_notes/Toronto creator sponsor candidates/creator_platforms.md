# Toronto Creator-Platform Sponsor Candidates

## Which companies are high-fit, reachable sponsor prospects?

### Takeaway

Nine companies meet the hard requirement: each is relevant to creators, design, social/media technology, education, audio/video, or AI creation; has Toronto/GTA or broader Canadian relevance; and publishes the exact retained email address on a company-owned webpage. The best first wave is Wattpad, Hootsuite, Voiceflow, Cohere, D2L, Thinkific, and Blackmagic Design because their official pages show particularly strong Toronto/Canadian ties and a plausible contribution matching the workshop.

### Inferences

- **Recommended outreach order:** Wattpad → Hootsuite → Voiceflow → Cohere → D2L → Thinkific → Blackmagic Design → Unity → Adobe. This prioritizes local presence and inbox relevance before company size.
- **Best cash prospects:** Wattpad, Hootsuite, D2L, Thinkific, and Adobe. This is an assessment based on scale, Canadian footprint, and alignment, not evidence of an active sponsorship budget.
- **Best in-kind prospects:** Blackmagic Design for devices/software; Adobe and Unity for licenses or training; Cohere and Voiceflow for platform credits and technical mentors.
- **Ask design:** Because the event is 17 days away, send a one-page package with three pre-priced options: CAD $3,500 cash; CAD $2,000 plus in-kind; or a fully in-kind package valued at CAD $3,000–$4,000. Offer named prize rights, logo placement, a judge seat, post-event photos, and an impact recap.
- **Routing discipline:** State “If this is not your team, could you forward this to partnerships/community/education/Canada marketing?” for support, PR, IR, or product-specific inboxes. Do not imply those addresses are sponsorship contacts.

### Gaps

- No retained company was found with an official page explicitly confirming a currently open sponsorship budget for this exact event type.
- Adobe, Unity, Cohere, Thinkific, and D2L publish qualifying official emails, but those inboxes are not dedicated sponsorship inboxes. Response and internal routing are uncertain.
- Blackmagic Design publishes a regional PR email covering Canada, not a Canada-only sponsorship email. Local product availability should be coordinated with an authorized GTA reseller only after corporate interest.
- Later, Descript, Riverside, Canva, Vimeo, Wacom, Shopify, and OpenAI were considered but not retained: research either did not establish a suitably visible official email, did not establish sufficient Canadian relevance, or surfaced only contacts too disconnected from sponsorship routing.
- Public email publication can change. Re-open each **Email_Source_URL** immediately before sending and preserve a screenshot or PDF of the visible address for the outreach record.
