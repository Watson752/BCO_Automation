# Gaming, Electronics, and Maker-Hardware Sponsor Candidates

## Which companies are high-fit, verifiable prospects for the EasyLens x Snapchat Toronto workshop/hackathon?

### Takeaway

Eight candidates passed the hard filter: each has Canadian or Toronto/GTA relevance and an exact email address visibly published on an official company-owned webpage. Best Buy Canada is the strongest first outreach because it publishes a dedicated sponsorship inbox; Canada Computers, Memory Express, and Henry's offer especially actionable local or category-specific contacts.

### Inferences

- **Recommended outreach order:** Best Buy Canada first because its published inbox is explicitly for sponsorships; Canada Computers and Henry's next because recent GTA workshops/events make the event format familiar; Memory Express next for a fast local in-kind decision; then ViewSonic, BenQ, Samsung, and Staples through business or media contacts.

- **Routing caveat:** Media and sales inboxes are qualifying official contacts but are not guaranteed sponsorship decision-makers. The opening line should explicitly ask the recipient to route the proposal internally if needed.
- **Capacity labels:** “High” and “Medium” are reasoned estimates based on company scale, programs, and documented hardware/event activity; they are not verified sponsorship budgets.
