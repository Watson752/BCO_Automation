# Toronto creator sponsor candidates — devices

## Which companies qualify?

### Takeaway
Nine unique-domain candidates qualify. Each has clear Canadian relevance, a strong phones/cameras/imaging/consumer-device fit, and an exact email visibly published on a company-owned webpage. Canon, Vistek, Henry's, Samsung, and Apple are the strongest first-wave targets; the remaining contacts are credible but less directly routed to sponsorship.

## How were candidates verified?

### Takeaway
Qualification was strict: the exact email had to appear in visible text on an official company-owned page, and the company needed separately verifiable Canadian relevance. No guessed addresses, third-party directories, contact forms without visible emails, or reseller emails were used as the candidate's primary email.

### Cited Findings
- Every retained row has a unique domain: canon.ca, vistek.ca, henrys.com, samsung.com, apple.com, lenovo.com, google.com, blackmagicdesign.com, and nikon.com.
- All exact emails were confirmed in full-page official-source fetches on 2026-10-26, not solely from search snippets.
- Canon's source is unusually strong because one page proves the email, Brampton headquarters, imaging category, and a September 2026 Ontario student-creator equipment partnership. — [Canon Canada](https://www.canon.ca/en/About-Canon/News/Press-Releases/2026/20260924-Laurier-Imaging-Partnership)
- Vistek's source directly pairs Toronto location and department-specific emails. — [Vistek](https://www.vistek.ca/about/contact)
- Apple's source supplies named contacts and exact emails on the Canadian newsroom. — [Apple Canada](https://www.apple.com/ca/newsroom/2026/03/apple-to-celebrate-50-years-of-thinking-different/)
- Lenovo's current contact page is dated January 1, 2026 and explicitly covers “journalists, creators, partners, and more.” — [Lenovo](https://news.lenovo.com/media-contacts/)

### Inferences
- “Capacity” is a qualitative outreach-prioritization estimate based on company scale, local operations, and evidenced program capability; it is not a verified sponsorship budget.
- “Recommended_Contribution” is a proposal tailored to the event and company's products, not evidence that the company has committed or maintains a matching formal program.
- Media contacts were retained because the user allowed PR/media contacts and because each is an official, verifiable route; outreach should be concise and explicitly request internal routing.

### Gaps
- Direct inbox deliverability was not tested; publication on a current official page confirms provenance, not that a human will answer before the event.
- The event date is 17 days from verification, so procurement and legal review are material risks. Parallel outreach should begin immediately, with in-kind options presented before or alongside cash.
- Sony, Fujifilm, Panasonic, OM System, Leica, and OPPO were investigated but not retained because the located official email was poorly matched, privacy/compliance-only, non-Canadian, or insufficiently supported by a strong Canadian sponsorship route relative to the nine candidates above.
