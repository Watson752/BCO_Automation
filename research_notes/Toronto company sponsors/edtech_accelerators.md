## Outreach caveats and sequencing

### Takeaway
Start with highly relevant partnership mailboxes immediately and make the ask modular: cash, catering, prizes, software/compute credits, mentors, judges, or promotion. A clear response deadline and low-friction options are important given the short lead time.

### Cited Findings
- Top Hat publishes a dedicated partnerships mailbox and Toronto headquarters on one contact page. [Official contact page](https://tophat.com/company/contact-us/)
- Riipen publishes a strategic-partnerships mailbox and positions that team around large-scale employability programs. [Official strategic-partnerships page](https://www.riipen.com/meet-with-our-strategic-partners-team); [official partnerships page](https://www.riipen.com/partnerships)
- OneEleven explicitly invites corporate partners to contact `partners@oneeleven.com`. [Official insights page](https://oneeleven.com/insights/)
- TKS Toronto explicitly includes AI, company challenges, hackathons, mentors, and prototype presentations. [Official Toronto program page](https://www.tks.world/program/toronto)
- L-SPARK’s current AI accelerator explicitly offers compute credits and one-on-one advisory support to participating Canadian companies, supporting an in-kind-first ask. [Official TELUS Sovereign AI Accelerator page](https://www.l-spark.com/telus-sovereign-ai-accelerator/)

### Inferences
- **Email subject suggestion:** `Toronto AI creator workshop Nov 12 — partnership/prize opportunity`
- **Call to action:** Request a 15-minute decision call within 48 hours and present three selectable options: `$1,000 cash`, `$500–$750 food/prize support`, or `in-kind services + judge/mentor`.
- **Personalization order:** lead with the candidate-specific hook, quantify the 100-person audience, mention EasyLens x Snapchat, state the Nov. 12 date, and close with the modular ask.

### Gaps
- The organizer’s final venue, agenda, sponsor benefits, brand-usage permissions, and charitable/tax-receipt status were not provided; these details can materially affect sponsor decisions.
- Confirm whether Snapchat branding may appear in outbound sponsor materials before representing the event as an official Snapchat partnership.
