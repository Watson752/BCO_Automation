## How should outreach be sequenced?

### Takeaway
Send a tightly scoped first wave immediately to MaRS, Fasken, FreshBooks, BDO and DMZ, giving each a cash option and a lower-friction in-kind option. Follow within one business day with OneEleven, Ownr and Goodlawyer, then use a 48-hour follow-up because the event is only 17 days away.

### Cited Findings
- **Wave 1 — event and partnership routes:** MaRS publishes an event-sponsorship email, DMZ publishes a named Strategic Partnerships Manager, OneEleven publishes a partner inbox, and FreshBooks publishes a global partner-program inbox. [MaRS](https://www.marsdd.com/contact/); [DMZ](https://dmz.torontomu.ca/press-releases/dmz-partners-with-indigenous-prosperity-foundation-to-launch-indigenous-launchpad-for-entrepreneurs); [OneEleven](https://oneeleven.com/insights/); [FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com/en-gb/partner)
- **Wave 1 — exact professional fit:** Laura Fetter’s official Fasken profile explicitly spans the creator economy, AI and VR/AR, while BDO’s Toronto centre is set up for tailored events and experiential workshops. [Fasken](https://www.fasken.com/en/laura-fetter); [BDO](https://www.bdo.ca/about/news/bdo-launches-cybersecurity-and-digital-innovation-centre-in-toronto)
- **Wave 2 — package options:** Ownr and FreshBooks can be approached with a shared incorporation-plus-accounting prize concept, while Goodlawyer can supply Toronto-based AI/privacy legal expertise. [FreshBooks–Ownr](https://www.freshbooks.com/press/releases/canadian-entrepreneurs-can-start-manage-and-grow-their-business-faster-with-new-freshbooks-and-ownr-partnership); [Goodlawyer](https://www.goodlawyer.ca/)

### Inferences
- Use a concrete subject line such as “Toronto student AI workshop — $750 prize or in-kind partner, Nov. 12” to make the decision small and routable.
- Offer category exclusivity only to the first confirmed legal and accounting sponsors; this can add urgency without increasing the requested amount.
- Ask in-kind prospects for named deliverables—e.g. 100 meal units, three software/service prizes, two mentor hours or one judge—rather than an open-ended “support” request.

### Gaps
- Venue, organizer legal entity, tax-receipt eligibility, existing Snapchat/EasyLens commitments and sponsor-benefit inventory were not provided; these may materially affect approval.
- No public source establishes whether Snapchat brand-approval rules permit sponsor logo placement, so avoid promising brand placement until organizer approval is confirmed.
