# Toronto company sponsors: talent, coworking, and innovation

## Which companies are strong sponsor candidates for the EasyLens x Snapchat Toronto workshop/hackathon?

### Takeaway
Nine candidates meet the hard filters: each has a distinct domain, an active Toronto or Canadian presence verified on an official company-owned page, and an email explicitly published on an official page. The best first-wave targets are MaRS, StartWell, Centre for Social Innovation, and DMZ because their published contacts are explicitly tied to partnerships, sponsorships, or community collaboration.

### Inferences
- **Suggested outreach order:** (1) MaRS, (2) StartWell, (3) CSI, (4) DMZ, (5) Vector, (6) ventureLAB, (7) Randstad, (8) WeWork, (9) Adecco. This ranks explicit partnership/sponsorship contacts above general or media-routing contacts.
- **Package design:** Because the event is only 17 days away, offer a single $3,500 presenting sponsorship plus modular in-kind alternatives: food for 100, a prize pool, venue/AV, coworking passes, mentors/judges, or recruitment/portfolio services.
- **Best cash prospects:** Randstad, Adecco, and potentially Vector have the strongest inferred institutional capacity; however, direct routing risk is higher for Randstad and Adecco.
- **Best in-kind prospects:** StartWell and WeWork for space/AV/passes; CSI for venue/community promotion; DMZ and ventureLAB for mentors, judges, prizes, and participant recruitment.
- **Best strategic fit:** MaRS and DMZ combine Toronto credibility, startup programming, partnership infrastructure, and close audience alignment. StartWell has the strongest operational fit for a 50-100-person event.

### Gaps
- No official public sponsorship inbox was found for WeWork; only its official press email was available.
- Adecco’s official named marketing email is published on a 2019 page, so current ownership of that mailbox remains uncertain.
- Randstad’s current named contact is corporate communications rather than sponsorship, and its separate media channel is explicitly restricted to journalists.
- Published financial sponsorship budgets were not available for any candidate; all **Capacity** ratings are qualitative estimates based on footprint, partnership infrastructure, and available assets, not confirmed budgets.
- Event venue, current booked date availability, and internal approval timelines were not verified.
