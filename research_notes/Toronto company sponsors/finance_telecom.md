# EasyLens x Snapchat Toronto Sponsor Candidates — Finance and Telecom

## Which companies are strong sponsor candidates with verified Canadian presence and an officially published email?

### Takeaway
Seven candidates meet the hard requirements: each has a distinct domain, documented Toronto or Canadian presence, and an email visibly published on a live official company webpage. Interac and KOHO offer the most directly relevant inboxes; the other five publish media/PR contacts, so their caveats are noted.

### Inferences
- **Best first outreach order:** Interac, KOHO, Wealthsimple, Wise, EQB, TELUS, then Rogers. This ordering prioritizes directly relevant partnership/sponsorship inboxes, immediate 2026 hooks, and audience fit.
- Recommended amounts are planning judgments, not published company funding levels. Treat them as alternative asks and assemble **two to four sponsors** to reach the event’s **$3,000–$4,000 total** target rather than expecting every candidate to contribute.
- A concise outreach should lead with the November 12 date, 100-person Toronto audience, EasyLens x Snapchat format, exact requested contribution, sponsor visibility, and a reply deadline around November 2–4 because the event is only 17 days away.
- Media inboxes should receive a short routing request, not a press pitch: “Could you connect us with community investment, partnerships, events, innovation, or talent?”

### Gaps
- No official reviewed source disclosed sponsorship budgets, approval lead times, or guaranteed response times.
- KOHO’s official pages verified Canadian operations but did not establish a current Toronto office.
- Rogers had strong Toronto technology evidence but no current 2026 sponsorship program or deadline in the reviewed official source.
- TELUS’s newer 2026 release obfuscated its email in fetched text; the exact address retained above comes from a still-live official 2020 page and therefore carries a staleness risk.
- Wealthsimple, Wise, EQB, Rogers, and TELUS publish media/PR—not sponsorship-specific—emails, so internal routing is required.
