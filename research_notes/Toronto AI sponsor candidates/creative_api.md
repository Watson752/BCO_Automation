# Creative technology and API sponsor candidates

## Screening outcome

### Takeaway
Five companies meet the hard screen: strong creator/AI/device fit, credible Canada or Toronto relevance, and at least one exact email displayed on an official company-owned webpage. The recommended package totals approximately **$4,000 in stated retail/in-kind value**, with ElevenLabs supplying the largest credit component.

### Cited Findings
- ElevenLabs publishes a standard in-person hackathon sponsorship package and says applications usually receive a response in 1–2 business days; it also announced a Toronto office and a Canadian team in 2026. — [Hackathon package](https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates); [Canada launch](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/canada)
- Samsung Canada operates a creator program that supplies devices and event access, features Toronto creators, and publishes an exact program email. — [#TeamSamsung](https://www.samsung.com/ca/team-samsung/)
- Sony has its Canadian head office in Toronto and a Canadian professional-imaging program with loaner equipment, special-event access, and a published email. — [Canada head office](https://corporate.sony.ca/view/contact_info.htm); [Imaging PRO Support](https://www.sony.ca/en/electronics/support/articles/S1F1523)
- Blackmagic Design publishes a PR email specifically covering Canada and lists authorized Toronto-area resellers on its Canadian site. — [Regional PR contact](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/ca/company/offices); [Toronto-area resellers](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/ca/products/atemconverters/howtobuy/)
- Friends of Figma Toronto is currently running Fall 2026 programming for Toronto product-design students and interns; an official Figma-hosted chapter page publishes the chapter email. — [Fall 2026 event](https://friends.figma.com/events/details/figma-toronto-presents-fall-2026-minis-craft-club-w-zoe/); [Official email source](https://friends.figma.com/events/details/figma-toronto-presents-friends-of-figma-toronto-first-play-date-of-2021/)

### Inferences
- These five cover distinct, complementary sponsor lanes: voice API credits, mobile/AR devices, imaging/photography, video-production tools, and collaborative design/community.
- Outreach should lead with the concrete Nov. 12 deliverable and one narrowly scoped contribution per company; the 17-day lead time makes broad, open-ended sponsorship requests less likely to close.

### Gaps
- No company has publicly committed to this event; all capacity ratings below are prospect-quality judgments, not confirmed budgets.
- Published emails are valid official-page contacts, but some route to media, support, or a local community chapter rather than a dedicated sponsorship manager.

## Contribution plan, exclusions, and caveats

### Takeaway
The five recommended asks sum to approximately **$4,000**: ElevenLabs $2,200 credits, Samsung $700 device support, Sony $500 imaging support, Blackmagic $400 software/training, and Figma Toronto $200 food/community support. If ElevenLabs adds its published winner tiers, reduce or decline other in-kind items to preserve a strict total-value ceiling.

### Cited Findings
- ElevenLabs’ published all-participant tier alone is worth $22 × 100 attendees, before its higher-value winner packages. — [Official package](https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates)
- Canva was not retained despite strong fit: its official page says new sponsorship programs are currently closed, while its 2026 Community Fund is limited to active Canvassadors/Experts and requires applications at least four weeks before the event—longer than the remaining lead time. — [Partnership status and email](https://www.canva.com/help/canva-partnerships/); [Community Fund](https://www.canva.com/community/); [Fund terms](https://www.canva.com/policies/canva-community-fund-terms-and-conditions/)
- Adobe, Logitech, Unity, Getty Images/Unsplash, Replicate, and Shutterstock were screened but not retained because the official emails found were primarily media, support, legal, contributor, or general-purpose routes and their current Toronto-specific sponsorship evidence was weaker than the five selected candidates.

### Inferences
- Prioritize in this order: ElevenLabs, Samsung Canada, Figma Toronto, Sony Canada, Blackmagic Design. This balances probability of quick action with contribution size.
- For every outreach, put the exact ask, 100-person audience, Toronto venue context, Nov. 12 date, EasyLens × Snapchat complementarity, and a reply deadline in the first paragraph.

### Gaps
- The event venue, organizer legal entity, sponsorship benefits, and tax/receipt status were not supplied; these details may be required before cash or equipment can be approved.
- No official public response-time commitment was found for Samsung, Blackmagic, or Figma Toronto.
- The stated values for Samsung, Sony, Blackmagic, and Figma are recommended asks, not published package prices or confirmed offers.
