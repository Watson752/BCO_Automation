# API and startup sponsor candidates

## Which companies qualify?

### Takeaway
Seven companies pass the hard contact test: each has an exact email displayed on an official company-owned webpage. The strongest first-wave targets are Cohere for Toronto relevance, Modal for proven hackathon sponsorship, Pinecone for a direct startup-program inbox, and Weights & Biases for an explicit university-event pathway.

### Inferences

- A realistic combined ask is CAD $500 cash from each of seven prospects, totaling CAD $3,500, while treating API credits, mentors, and prizes as incremental. Because no company has publicly committed to this event, every amount above is a recommended ask rather than a forecast.
- The outreach order should be: Cohere, Modal, Pinecone, Weights & Biases, AssemblyAI, Deepgram, Together AI. This prioritizes local relevance, direct program inboxes, and documented event sponsorship ahead of generic support-routing addresses.
- Credits should be packaged as optional challenge infrastructure, not the only participant benefit. A 100-person mixed audience will include designers and creators who may not use every API.

### Gaps

- No reviewed page confirms that any candidate has budget reserved for the Nov. 12, 2026 event.
- Except for Cohere, no verified Toronto office or current Toronto event was found.
- Public support emails for Modal, AssemblyAI, Deepgram, and Together AI are valid official addresses but are not sponsorship-specific; internal routing may reduce response speed.
- Canadian program eligibility is inferred only where public eligibility pages omit geographic exclusions. Event organizers should confirm credit redemption and prize-tax requirements in Canada before announcing benefits.

## How should outreach be prioritized?

### Takeaway
Send a same-day first wave to Cohere, Modal, Pinecone, and Weights & Biases, then a voice/compute second wave to AssemblyAI, Deepgram, and Together AI. Use a one-page, low-friction menu with CAD $500 cash as the default and credits, prizes, judging, or mentorship as substitutes.

### Cited Findings

- Modal has the clearest comparable sponsorship precedent: $250 in compute credits per participant and a $5,000 choice award in a prior AI hackathon. ([Agents & MCP Hackathon](https://huggingface.co/Agents-MCP-Hackathon))
- Pinecone supplies a direct startup-program email and has previously organized a 1,400+ participant hackathon with more than $100,000 in credits and prizes. ([startup program](https://www.pinecone.io/startup-program/); [hackathon recap](https://www.pinecone.io/blog/the-first-pinecone-hackathon/))
- W&B explicitly invites universities to contact it about hosting events, making its research inbox unusually well aligned with student outreach. ([research and education](https://wandb.ai/site/research/))
- Cohere is the only retained company with verified Toronto headquarters, making it the best candidate for an in-person representative even though its available email is a routing address. ([SaaS agreement](https://cohere.com/saas-agreement))

### Inferences

- Use the subject line: **Toronto Nov. 12: $500 sponsor track for 100 AI creators**. The small, specific ask and fixed date make internal forwarding easier.
- Set a response deadline of Oct. 30 for cash and Nov. 3 for digital credits/prizes. Offer logo placement, a named challenge, a judge seat, a two-minute welcome, and an opt-in participant follow-up as the standard return.
- Do not promise category exclusivity unless a sponsor contributes a meaningful share of the total cash target.

### Gaps

- The organizer’s legal entity, venue, exact agenda, Snapchat’s confirmed role, tax treatment, and sponsor-benefit inventory were not provided; these details will affect approval.
- No named individual sponsorship or developer-relations contact with an exact publicly displayed company email was found for most retained companies.
