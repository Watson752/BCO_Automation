# Toronto and Canadian technology sponsor candidates

Verified 2026-10-26 for an EasyLens x Snapchat Toronto workshop/hackathon on 2026-11-12.

## Method and qualification gate

The retained list is limited to commercial companies with:

- a Toronto or material Canadian presence supported by an official company-owned page;
- a credible connection to students, designers, creators, developers, or AI builders;
- capacity to make a modest cash or in-kind contribution;
- an exact email address explicitly published on an official company-owned webpage.

No address was inferred from a naming pattern. The contact addresses below are mostly press or communications channels; outreach should politely ask that the proposal be routed to community, developer relations, partnerships, brand, or field marketing. Recommended asks are deliberately small and divisible so the event can reach a total sponsor value of approximately CAD $3,000–$4,000 without relying on one company.

## Suggested outreach order and ask structure

1. **Cohere and Shopify:** strongest direct platform/creator alignment; ask first for credits, food, prizes, and a technical representative.
2. **Wealthsimple and 1Password:** strongest young-builder/product and secure-development hooks; make a narrowly branded prize or clinic request.
3. **Waabi, Xanadu, and BenchSci:** distinctive deep-tech partners; prioritize a mentor/judge plus a small prize or food contribution.

A practical package is two CAD $750 lead asks, three CAD $500 supporting asks, and two CAD $300–$500 in-kind/challenge asks. Accepting a subset keeps total realized support near the CAD $3,000–$4,000 objective.
