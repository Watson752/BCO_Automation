# Prioritize Routable Toronto Sponsors Before November
The final shortlist contains **exactly 18 candidates with 18 unique normalized domains**, selected for fast, verifiable outreach before the Nov. 12, 2026 EasyLens x Snapchat Toronto workshop. The strongest first wave is Interac, Top Hat, OneEleven, MaRS, KOHO, and StartWell because each publishes a relevant sponsorship, partnership, or founder-level mailbox on an official page ([Interac](https://www.interac.ca/en/content/contact-media-team/); [Top Hat](https://tophat.com/company/contact-us/); [OneEleven](https://oneeleven.com/insights/); [MaRS](https://hubs.marsdd.com/contact/); [KOHO](https://www.koho.ca/contact/); [StartWell](https://startwell.co/pages/partnership)). The selection covers banks and fintech, telecom, recruiting and talent, coworking, education technology, company-operated accelerators, startup law and accounting, and Toronto's innovation ecosystem. A realistic path to the **$3,000-$4,000 total goal** is one $1,000 anchor, two $500-$750 contributors, and in-kind food, space, prizes, services, or mentors. No candidate has publicly committed funds, so every contribution and capacity estimate is an outreach recommendation rather than a confirmed offer.

## Official emails determined every inclusion
All four research-note files were reviewed, then each selected email was checked against its exact company-owned source page. No address was inferred from a naming convention, copied only from a third party, or accepted from a contact form. Named contacts appear only where the official page explicitly identifies the person and role, including Fasken's Laura Fetter, StartWell's Qasim Virjee, Randstad's Chahinez Dib, and TELUS's Brandi Rees ([Fasken](https://www.fasken.com/en/laura-fetter); [StartWell](https://startwell.co/pages/partnership); [Randstad](https://www.randstad.ca/newsroom/randstad-canada-announces-leadership-transition-new-appointments/); [TELUS](https://www.telus.com/en/about/news-and-events/media-releases/telus-ranked-as-canadas-fastest-mobile-network-and-canadas-fastest-internet-provider)). Toronto or Canadian presence was retained only when supported by the notes and an official source; Toronto headquarters or offices strengthened priority, while Canada-only candidates such as KOHO and Riipen are described without implying a Toronto office.

The shortlist balances **three fintech candidates, two telecoms, one recruiting company, two coworking companies, three edtech companies, two company-operated accelerator/ecosystem companies, three startup legal or accounting providers, one major innovation hub, and one startup-formation platform**. OneEleven and Highline Beta satisfy both the company-operated accelerator and Toronto innovation-ecosystem objectives, while MaRS adds the clearest event-sponsorship route: its official page labels `partnerships@marsdd.com` for partnerships, corporate programming, or event sponsorship ([MaRS](https://hubs.marsdd.com/contact/)). Interac is similarly actionable because `community@interac.ca` is expressly designated for sponsorship and community investment ([Interac](https://www.interac.ca/en/content/contact-media-team/)).

## Modular asks improve the 17-day odds
Cash requests were kept mostly between **$500 and $1,500 per candidate** so the total can be assembled across several approvals rather than depending on a single sponsor. StartWell and WeWork are primarily in-kind venue, production, and coworking-pass prospects; Rogers and TELUS are stronger for connectivity, devices, services, or technical mentors; Fasken, FreshBooks, BDO, and Ownr can contribute legal, accounting, cybersecurity, or incorporation services. StartWell is operationally well matched because it advertises weekly 100-person events and integrated venue, AV, media, and hospitality services ([StartWell](https://startwell.co/pages/partnership)). Top Hat, Riipen, and D2L offer direct student and learning alignment, with Top Hat and Riipen providing the most relevant partnership mailboxes ([Top Hat](https://tophat.com/company/contact-us/); [Riipen](https://www.riipen.com/meet-with-our-strategic-partners-team)).

Outreach should lead with the fixed Nov. 12 date, the 100-person Toronto audience, one candidate-specific hook, and two selectable options: a precise cash amount or a concrete in-kind package. Wealthsimple deserves immediate but carefully routed outreach because its Toronto product showcase occurs Nov. 5 and features product, design, engineering, and AI leadership, exactly one week before the workshop ([Wealthsimple](https://newsroom.wealthsimple.com/media-advisory-wealthsimple-presents-something-good-is-going-to-happen)). A 48-hour response request is reasonable, but the public sources do not establish decision times or sponsorship budgets.

## Routing and staleness remain material caveats
Several valid emails are not sponsorship channels. Wealthsimple, Rogers, TELUS, D2L, and WeWork publish press or media addresses, while Randstad publishes a current corporate-communications contact; these records are labeled accordingly and should receive a transparent request for referral to partnerships, community, brand, talent, or local leadership. TELUS's exact unmasked address is on a live official 2020 page, and Ownr's `hello@ownr.co` plus Toronto-location source was last updated in 2023, so both should be tested early rather than treated as sole channels. FreshBooks' partnerships email is published on a UK-localized official page, although that page states the program accepts partners internationally ([FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com/en-gb/partner)).

Adecco was not selected even though the research notes cited an official 2019 email source: the URL now resolves to a general page whose fetched content does not display the named email, so it no longer met the strict publication test. MaRS is included for its uniquely explicit sponsorship route but is a mission-driven innovation organization rather than a conventional for-profit company. No source confirms current venue availability, cash approval authority, tax-receipt eligibility, inbox deliverability, or permission to offer Snapchat logo placement; sponsor benefits should therefore avoid unapproved brand promises.

## Conclusion
The best near-term strategy is to contact the six directly routable first-wave candidates immediately while sending media-address candidates short referral requests in parallel. The shortlist's value is not that every lead will fund the event, but that every row has a traceable official email, a supported Canadian presence, and a contribution sized to a fast, modular close.
